# The common-practice vocabulary of a 12-note chromatic:
# 12 major keys, 24 major and minor triads, 12 dominant sevenths.
#
# Slot i is the i-th chromatic step, slot 0 the tonic. Sub-scales are of
# mixed size (3, 4 and 7 notes); each is scored on its own terms and they
# count equally, so a triad is not penalised for being smaller than a key.
#
# The triads and sevenths are subsets of the keys, so they add no new
# restriction on which notes may sound together -- they weight the harmonies
# that matter most, rather than constraining the collection further.
