# The 12 major keys as sub-scales of a 12-note chromatic.
#
# Slot i is the i-th chromatic step, slot 0 the tonic. Each line is one
# diatonic set {0,2,4,5,7,9,11} transposed. Its 12 rotations are the 12 keys;
# the modes of a key are the same pitch-class set, so they are not listed
# separately.
#
# Adjacent keys on the circle of fifths share 6 of 7 notes. In 12-TET all 12
# are identical by construction; in just intonation they cannot be, so this
# asks how evenly the compromise can be spread -- the search is effectively
# looking for a well-temperament in JI.
 0  2  4  5  7  9 11   # C major
 0  1  3  5  6  8 10   # Db major
 1  2  4  6  7  9 11   # D major
 0  2  3  5  7  8 10   # Eb major
 1  3  4  6  8  9 11   # E major
 0  2  4  5  7  9 10   # F major
 1  3  5  6  8 10 11   # Gb major
 0  2  4  6  7  9 11   # G major
 0  1  3  5  7  8 10   # Ab major
 1  2  4  6  8  9 11   # A major
 0  2  3  5  7  9 10   # Bb major
 1  3  4  6  8 10 11   # B major
