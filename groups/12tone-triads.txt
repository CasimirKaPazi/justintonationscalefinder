# The 24 major and minor triads of a 12-note chromatic.
#
# Slot i is the i-th chromatic step, slot 0 the tonic. Major triad is
# {0,4,7} transposed, minor {0,3,7}. This asks for a 12-note scale in which
# every common-practice triad is consonant, ignoring scale membership.
#
# A stricter demand than the keys file in one way -- 24 constraints rather
# than 12 -- and looser in another, since it says nothing about the
# seven-note collections those triads live in.
